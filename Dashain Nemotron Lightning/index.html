The Story of Dashain

1

Victory of Good Over Evil

Long ago, the gods battled the demon Mahishasura. After nine nights of fierce fight, Goddess Durga emerged victorious. Dashain marks that divine triumph — light overpowering darkness.

2

Tika and Jamara

Elders bless the younger with tika (a mixture of rice, yogurt and vermilion) and jamara (young barley shoots). The tika marks the forehead, the jamara sprouts from the earth — a living symbol of growth, protection and ancestral love.

3

Family reunion

People travel from near and far to come home. Sisters put tika on brothers' foreheads, families share feasts, and every heart overflows with gratitude. The bamboo swing (ping) sways, and kites fill the sky.

4

Lighting the diya

Each evening, a diya (oil lamp) is lit to welcome prosperity and ward off darkness. Its flame whispers: hope endures, day follows night.
