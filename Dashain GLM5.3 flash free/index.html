Chapter One · The Victory

Good Conquers Evil

Dashain begins with the oldest story of light defeating darkness — the fifteen days of the great battle, ending on the tenth day, Vijaya Dashami, the day of victory. It reminds us that patience, courage, and goodness outlast any mighty sorrow.

Chapter Two · The Blessing

Tika & Jamara

On Dashami you bow to your elders and receive a rice-dipped red tika — each grain a prayer for your long life — and a sprig of yellow jamara, grown in darkness and brought to light for umbilical-style freshness of hope. Elders change your bag with their love, not gold.

"May every step you take lead to light." 🙏

Chapter Three · The Swing

The Ping

In every village square a bamboo swing rises — the ping. Children swing higher and higher, and elders remember the smell of the earth after the monsoon. Dashain is that feeling: to be carried awhile, and to return smiling.
